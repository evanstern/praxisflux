# Decision Briefing — plan-timeline

**What this page answers:** Lay out the plan or rollout as a sequence of phases or dates.

**Facts it needs:** every claim on this page must trace to an id in
`facts.json` (or, for a generated page, to the generator's own output) —
never state something the fact-gatherer didn't record.

**Patterns that fit:** `steps`.
**Diagrams that fit:** `timeline`.

**House style:** see `references/house-style.md` for the full text of
`svg-diagrams-not-ascii`, `sub-pages-not-scroll`.

**What to avoid:** Don't leave the plan as prose — a timeline or steps diagram makes the sequence checkable.
