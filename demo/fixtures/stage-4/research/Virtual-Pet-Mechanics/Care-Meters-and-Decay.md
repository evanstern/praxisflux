# Care Meters and Decay

Tamagotchi-lineage pets are built on a small set of visible meters — canonically Hunger
and Happiness rendered as hearts — that decay on timers and are refilled by distinct
care verbs. Modern virtual-pet templates widen the set (Hunger, Happiness, Cleanliness,
Energy) but keep the same shape: every stat decays over time, every verb trades between
stats.

## What is known

- Hunger depletes continuously and is refilled by meals; an empty meter triggers an
  attention call ([[_grounding]] — care meters).
- Happiness is refilled through play, snacks, toys, and social connection; snack
  overfeeding is deliberately punished (toothache → medicine), so the cheap refill
  carries a cost ([[_grounding]] — care meters).
- Decay accelerates with age on the original hardware, capping at one Hungry heart per
  6 minutes and one Happy heart per 7 — the game gets more demanding as the pet matures
  ([[_grounding]] — care meters).
- Refill/decay asymmetry is the core balance dial: verbs that raise one meter typically
  tax another, so a player cannot max everything with one action.
