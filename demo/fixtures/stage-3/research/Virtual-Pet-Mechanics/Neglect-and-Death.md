# Neglect and Death

Virtual pets make neglect legible through "care mistakes" and make sustained neglect
fatal — or deliberately don't. Where a game sits on that spectrum is one of its defining
design choices.

## What is known

- A care mistake is an unanswered attention call within a fixed window (typically 15
  minutes on Tamagotchi hardware); calls fire at zeroed meters or sickness
  ([[_grounding]] — care mistakes).
- Mistake categories: hunger, sickness, neglect. Accumulated mistakes shorten life and
  shape evolution; severe neglect kills prematurely ([[_grounding]] — care mistakes).
- Original-line lifespans averaged ~12 in-game days (≈7 low, up to ~25 high); good care
  earns a "good end" (an egg laid before death) while neglect yields the bad end
  ([[_grounding]] — care mistakes).
- The industry spectrum of death handling: soft death (faint, pay/act to revive),
  retirement (aged pets move to a gallery), and permadeath from neglect with no revive
  ([[_grounding]] — care mistakes).
