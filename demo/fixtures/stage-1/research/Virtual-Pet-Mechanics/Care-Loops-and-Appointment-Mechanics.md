# Care Loops and Appointment Mechanics

The virtual-pet care loop is an appointment mechanic: decaying stats set a cadence of
required returns, and the pet's welfare is the stake. This note maps what is known about
why that loop works and where it fails.

## What is known

- An appointment mechanic is anything telling the player "come back later", turning play
  into routine; timers with capped accumulation set the return interval
  ([[_grounding]] — retention).
- Pet framing strengthens the appointment: returning is a promise to someone else, not
  to yourself — a "moral obligation to return" ([[_grounding]] — retention).
- The stakes need a middle setting: a pet that can die is harsh, one that can't be
  affected is boring; suffering-but-recoverable creates stakes and hope together
  ([[_grounding]] — retention).
- Failure mode: the "virtual pet treadmill", where the pet only punishes neglect —
  players eventually resent the obligation ([[_grounding]] — retention).
- Appointment-driven idle titles show elevated stickiness in industry analyses (≈18%
  vs ≈10.5% for comparable hyper-casual games) ([[_grounding]] — retention).
